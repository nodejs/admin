# Discord Management Policy

The Node.js Discord server (available at [discord.gg/nodejs][]) is the
official community Discord for the Node.js project. This document
describes how the server is managed, who is responsible for it, and the
policies that govern its operation.

## The Official Node.js Discord Server

The Node.js Discord server is an official community space for the
Node.js project. It originated as the Nodeiflux community server,
created in 2018, and was adopted as the official Node.js Discord server
by the Node.js Technical Steering Committee (TSC) in March 2025 through
a partnership with the existing volunteer management team.

The Discord server is owned and operated by the Discord management team.
The Node.js project does not own the server. The official designation is
a recognition granted by the Node.js project that the server serves as
the primary community Discord space for Node.js users, subject to the
terms of this policy.

The server carries Discord's verified status and is linked from the
Node.js website. This official designation does not constitute an
endorsement by the Node.js project or the OpenJS Foundation of any
content, event, or external product or service promoted within the
server, unless explicitly stated otherwise.

If the TSC determines that this policy is not being upheld, it may
revoke the server's official status by a standard TSC vote. In that
case, the Node.js project will remove any official references to the
server, including from the Node.js website and any verified status
arrangements. The server itself continues to exist under the management
team's ownership and is free to operate independently.

## Purpose

The Node.js Discord server serves as a community space for end users,
developers, contributors, and enthusiasts. Its purposes include:

* Providing a place for Node.js users to ask questions, discuss the
  runtime, and share projects.
* Mirroring official Node.js project announcements, including release
  notifications and events organized by the OpenJS Foundation or the
  Node.js project.
* Hosting livestreams and community events by Node.js Ambassadors,
  contributors, and community members.
* Connecting members of the broader community with Node.js contributors
  in an informal, accessible setting.

The Discord server is not a venue for Node.js project governance,
technical decision-making, or Collaborator coordination. Those
activities continue to take place on GitHub and in the OpenJS Foundation
Slack. Node.js Collaborators are not required to participate in the
Discord server.

## Relationship to the Node.js Project

The Node.js Discord server operates under the following governance
structure:

* **Discord management team** — The volunteer team responsible for
  day-to-day management of the server, including channel structure, role
  assignments, bot configuration, and routine community moderation. The
  management team has autonomy over day-to-day operational decisions
  within the limits of this policy.

* **Node.js TSC** — The TSC holds ultimate responsibility for the
  server's official designation as a Node.js community space. The TSC
  must be kept informed of significant decisions and must approve any
  matter that falls outside the scope of routine operations, including
  decisions covered by this policy. The TSC serves as the final
  escalation point and may, by a standard TSC vote, revoke the server's
  official status. Revocation does not affect the management team's
  ownership of the server.

* **OpenJS Foundation** — Matters involving the use of OpenJS Foundation
  or Node.js trademarks in a commercial context, or that implicate
  Foundation interests, require involvement of the OpenJS Foundation
  staff in addition to TSC awareness. The OpenJS Cross Project Council
  (CPC) serves as the escalation path for disputes that cannot be
  resolved by the TSC.

The management team must communicate significant decisions and changes to
the TSC on an ongoing basis. Where this policy requires TSC or Foundation
involvement, the management team must open an issue in the
[Node.js admin repository][nodejs/admin] to initiate that process before
the relevant decision is made or action taken.

## Management and Administration

The Discord management team has full autonomy to operate the server as
it sees fit. This includes decisions about channel structure, roles,
permissions, moderation tools, bots and integrations, event scheduling,
and all other aspects of the server's day-to-day operation. The TSC
does not require notice of or approval for routine operational decisions.

The management team is responsible for:

* Maintaining the server's channel structure, roles, and permissions.
* Recruiting, onboarding, and removing server moderators and
  administrators.
* Enforcing the Code of Conduct within the server.
* Managing bots and integrations used in the server.
* Communicating significant changes to the TSC as appropriate.

Any changes to the server's name, logo, branding, or verified status —
or any other change to the way the server presents itself as the
official Node.js Discord — require involvement of the OpenJS Foundation
before taking effect. The management team must open an issue in the
[Node.js admin repository][nodejs/admin] to initiate that review.

TSC voting members and Node.js Moderation Team members may be granted
elevated roles in the Discord server to enable oversight and emergency
moderation. The management team maintains a direct communication channel
with the Node.js Moderation Team to facilitate coordination.

The management team must maintain an audit log of administrative actions
taken within the server, available to TSC voting members and Node.js
Moderation Team members upon request.

## Code of Conduct

The Node.js Discord server follows the [Node.js Code of Conduct][CoC],
which incorporates the [OpenJS Foundation Code of Conduct][OpenJS CoC].
All participants in the server are expected to abide by this Code of
Conduct. The Code of Conduct applies to all channels, threads, and
direct interactions that take place within the server.

## Moderation

Day-to-day moderation is carried out by the Discord management team's
volunteer moderation staff. The Node.js Moderation Team maintains
elevated permissions in the server and may intervene in moderation
matters, particularly in cases of escalation or where the management
team's moderation capacity is insufficient.

Reports of Code of Conduct violations in the Discord server may be
submitted to the [report-discord@nodejs.org][] email address. Email sent
to this address reaches both the Discord moderation staff and the
Node.js Moderation Team. Reports may also be submitted by private
message to any Discord administrator or any Node.js Moderation Team
member.

Moderation decisions may be escalated to the Node.js Moderation Team
and, further, to the OpenJS Foundation Code of Conduct Team following
the [escalation process][escalation] defined by the OpenJS Foundation.

## Community Events

The Discord management team may organize and host community events,
including livestreams, Q&A sessions, Ask Me Anything events, and other
community-organized programming.

Events that are not organized by or on behalf of the Node.js project or
the OpenJS Foundation must be clearly identified as community events
rather than official Node.js project events. Any announcement or
description of such an event must make clear that it is not organized by
or endorsed by the Node.js project or the OpenJS Foundation.

Community events organized by or in partnership with a third party —
including companies or individuals not otherwise affiliated with the
Node.js project — must be disclosed to the TSC before being announced in
the server. The management team must open an issue in the
[Node.js admin repository][nodejs/admin] to initiate this disclosure
and allow the TSC and OpenJS Foundation staff to raise any concerns.

## Sponsorships and Commercial Arrangements

The Discord management team operates the server independently and may
enter into arrangements to support its operation. The requirements for
those arrangements depend on whether they involve the use of the
official Node.js name, logo, or verified server status.

### Arrangements not involving the Node.js brand

Commercial arrangements that are not presented as being associated with
the Node.js project and that do not make commercial use of the Node.js
or OpenJS Foundation name, logo, or the server's official status are
the management team's own business. The TSC does not require advance
notice or approval for such arrangements.

### Arrangements involving the Node.js brand

Any commercial arrangement — including sponsorships, paid partnerships,
and event monetization — that makes use of the Node.js name, logo, or
the server's "official" status as a commercial selling point is subject
to the following requirements:

* The management team must open an issue in the [Node.js admin
  repository][nodejs/admin] disclosing the proposed arrangement before
  entering into it. This allows the TSC and OpenJS Foundation staff to
  confirm the arrangement does not conflict with existing Foundation
  partner relationships or otherwise implicate Foundation interests.
* The arrangement must not claim or imply endorsement of any product,
  service, or organization by the Node.js project or the OpenJS
  Foundation.
* Any event, content, or communication associated with a sponsorship
  must be clearly labeled as sponsored content and must include a
  statement that it is not organized by or endorsed by the Node.js
  project or the OpenJS Foundation.
* If the TSC or Foundation staff determines that the arrangement
  conflicts with Foundation interests, the management team must not
  proceed with the arrangement.

Revenue from commercial arrangements is retained by the management team
and does not pass through the OpenJS Foundation. If the management team
wishes to enter into a commercial partnership in an official capacity on
behalf of the Node.js project, it should contact the OpenJS Foundation
to discuss appropriate channels, such as the [Ecosystem Sustainability
Program][ESP].

## Notifications and Communications

The management team may use role-based notifications to communicate
announcements to server members. The following requirements apply:

* Mass notifications using @everyone or @here are reserved for official
  Node.js project communications, such as release announcements or
  events organized by the Node.js project or the OpenJS Foundation.
  They must not be used for community events, sponsored content, or any
  communication that is not organized by or on behalf of the Node.js
  project or the OpenJS Foundation.
* Community events must use opt-in role pings only. Members who have
  not explicitly subscribed to a notification role must not receive
  notifications about community or sponsored events.
* Events associated with commercial sponsorships must not use any mass
  notification mechanism, including @everyone, @here, or any opt-in
  notification role whose primary stated purpose is receiving Node.js
  project announcements.

## Node.js Collaborator Participation

Node.js Collaborators, Triagers, and Ambassadors who participate in the
Discord server may request roles that identify their affiliation with the
Node.js project. Such roles are available at the discretion of the
management team. Participation in the Discord server is voluntary; it is
not a requirement for any Node.js project role.

Collaborators and Triagers who participate in the Discord server are
expected to uphold the same standards of conduct expected in all other
Node.js community spaces.

## Modifications to This Policy

Modifications to this policy are subject to approval by the TSC. When
modifications are proposed, if there are no objections from TSC members
within 72 hours, the modifications are accepted. If any objection is
made, a TSC vote in favor of the change is required.
